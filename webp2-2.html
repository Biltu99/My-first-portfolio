🎯 Tips for Visiting Durga Puja Pandals

Best Timing

The best nights to visit are on Durga Puja evening (the main night) and Bhai Dooj (the following night). The crowds are largest between 9:00 PM and 1:00 AM.

Transport

Avoid private cars as roads are blocked. The Kolkata Metro is the best way to travel. Use app-based taxis or autos for last-mile connectivity.

Safety

Keep your valuables secure in crowded places. Carry minimal cash and avoid wearing expensive jewelry during visits.

Local Guides

Don't hesitate to ask locals for directions. Kolkatans are very helpful during the puja season and love sharing their cultural knowledge.

Stay Connected

Keep your phone charged and share your location with family. Download offline maps in case of network congestion.

Food & Water

Stay hydrated and try traditional Bengali sweets available at pandals. Street food is abundant but choose clean vendors.
